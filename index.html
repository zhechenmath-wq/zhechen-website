Professional Summary

I am currently a research and teaching assistant at Université Paris Cité. I completed my PhD at Sorbonne University under the supervision of Prof. Frédérique Charles (Université Grenoble Alpes) and Prof. François Golse (École Polytechnique). My research focuses on kinetic theory and the hydrodynamic limit. I am currently seeking postdoctoral opportunities. Please feel free to contact me regarding relevant opportunities. Thank you!
